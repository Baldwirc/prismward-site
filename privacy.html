Privacy Policy

Effective November 15, 2026

Prismward is a mobile game. This policy explains what the game collects, why, and how you can delete it. In short: no name, email, contacts, location or advertising ID is needed to play. What we collect is about how the game is played, and it isn’t sold or used to track you across other apps.

What we collect

Created automatically when you play

  • A guest account. The game creates an anonymous account for you, identified only by a random ID. It holds your game progress (levels, upgrades, currencies, research, daily missions) so it’s saved on our server.
  • Gameplay events. Things like starting and finishing a battle, the stage and result, upgrades, research, tutorial tips shown, idle earnings collected, settings changed, and how long a session lasts. Each is tagged with a random install ID, your game level, the device type (iOS or Android) and the app version.
  • Battle records. For each battle, the server stores the battle’s random seed and the taps you made, so it can replay the battle and confirm the result is real.
  • Crash and performance reports. If the game hits an error or stops responding, it records the error message, technical details, and the last few game actions before it happened.

Only if you choose to send it

  • Feedback. The message, rating and optional name you type into the Feedback form, plus what screen you were on.
  • Transfer codes. If you move your progress to another phone, we store the one-time code and when it was used.

Purchases

Payments are handled entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never see your card or payment details. To confirm a purchase and add it to your game, we receive the purchase’s transaction ID, the item bought, the date and the store, through our purchase service RevenueCat.

What we don’t collect

We don’t collect your name (unless you type it into feedback), email address, phone number, contacts, photos, precise or approximate location, or your device’s advertising ID. Reminders are scheduled on your phone itself; no notification token is sent to us.

How we use it

  • To run the game and save your progress.
  • To check battles and purchases, so rewards can’t be faked.
  • To find and fix crashes and bugs.
  • To balance and improve the game (for example, seeing where players get stuck).
  • To read and respond to feedback.

We don’t sell your data, share it with advertisers, or use it to track you across other companies’ apps and websites. The game currently shows no third-party ads. If that changes, this policy will be updated first.

Who processes it for us

  • Supabase stores game data and runs our game server.
  • RevenueCat confirms purchases with Apple and Google.
  • Apple and Google process payments and deliver the app.

They process data only to provide these services to us.

How long we keep it

Your game data is kept while your account exists. When you delete your data (below), we delete your account, progress, battle records, transfer codes, gameplay events and feedback. We keep a record of purchases (transaction ID, item, date and store, no longer linked to your account) because we need it for refunds and tax records.

Deleting your data

In the game, tap the gear, then Delete my data. It’s immediate and permanent, and you start over as a new player. You can also email us to ask for deletion; include your game’s transfer code or the approximate date you started playing so we can find your data.

Children

Prismward isn’t directed at children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information (for example, through feedback), contact us and we’ll delete it.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections. The app can read only your own saved progress, and can only add play events and feedback, never read them back. Only the game server can change progress.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the date above, and for significant changes we’ll tell players in the game.
