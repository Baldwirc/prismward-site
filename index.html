Prismward

Restore a ruined sun sanctuary. Route sunlight through prisms and mirrors, and turn one ray into a screen-clearing rainbow cascade.

Support

The fastest way to reach us is the Feedback button at the top right of the game’s home screen. It sends your message straight to the developer along with what screen you were on.

You can also email prismwardgames@outlook.com.

Common questions

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

On your old phone, tap the gear → Get a code. On the new phone, install Prismward, tap the gear → Enter a code, and type it in. The code works once, for 24 hours. Your old phone starts fresh afterward.

Something I bought is missing.

Open the Shop and tap Restore purchases. If it’s still missing, use Feedback or email us with the date of the purchase; you won’t be charged twice.

How do I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by the store you bought from. On iPhone, visit reportaproblem.apple.com. On Android, see Google Play’s refund help.

How do I turn off sound, music, vibration or reminders?

Tap the gear at the top right of the home screen.

How do I delete my data?

Tap the gear → Delete my data. See the privacy policy for what that removes.
